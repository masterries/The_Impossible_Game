The Impossible Game

A remake of World’s Hardest Game · 12 levels · no checkpoints

Turn your phone sideways for a bigger playfield.

Drag anywhere on the field to steer. Sound starts after your first input.

Modes

Campaign and sudden death have their own scoreboard. Practice a single level below; it is never ranked.

Controls

Arrow keys / W A S D Move
Drag on the field Move (touch)
Space or tap Confirm a dialog
R Restart the level
P / Esc Pause

Rules

  • You are the red cube.
  • Collect every coin in the level.
  • Only then does the green goal open.
  • Touching blue sends you back.
  • Striped tiles drag you along.
  • Red doors open and close in turns.
  • Matching rings teleport you.
